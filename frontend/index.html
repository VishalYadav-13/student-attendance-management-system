Ethical Biometrics & Gemini AI Integration

Designed responsibly: SAMS does not store raw student camera frames or rely blindly on free-form LLM outputs.

1. Client Camera & Liveness

Student explicitly grants camera access. The client guides the student through positioning and liveness validation cues.

2. Server-Side Gemini Quality

The backend verifies lighting, blurriness, and single-face presence via Gemini API without exposing credentials to the client.

3. Biometric Feature Match

Mathematical landmark vectors are compared against enrolled student profiles with 0.75+ confidence thresholds and manual teacher fallback.
